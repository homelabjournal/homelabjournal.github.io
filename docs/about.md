# About

We're a dad and his teenage son building a home lab together, in a 9U wall cabinet in a cupboard.

He's been coding for 30-odd years; his son is a teenager, learning by building. The plan runs from a Pi-hole on a Raspberry Pi from 2012 all the way to a firewall, a small Kubernetes cluster, Minecraft for his friends, a VPN, and cameras that don't phone home.

We write about what we tried, what broke and what we'd do differently. We don't publish our addresses, network details or anything that would help someone get into the house. We keep that bit to ourselves.
