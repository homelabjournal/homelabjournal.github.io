# About

I'm a dad building a home lab with my teenage son, in a 9U wall cabinet in a cupboard.

I've been coding for 30-odd years; he's learning by building. Our plan runs from a Pi-hole on a Raspberry Pi from 2012 all the way to a firewall, a small Kubernetes cluster, Minecraft for his friends, a VPN, and cameras that don't phone home.

We're doing it with a lot of help from AI: the plan was written with Claude, the code is written with Claude Code, and these posts are drafted with Claude from our notes and then edited by us. We review and understand everything before it runs in our house.

I write about what we tried, what broke and what we'd do differently. I don't publish our addresses, network details or anything that would help someone get into the house. That bit stays with us.
